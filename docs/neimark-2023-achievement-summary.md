# NEIMARK 2023 - Achievement Summary

## Event

In 2023, I took part in NEIMARK educational initiatives in Nizhny Novgorod related to project work, mentoring, and AI ethics.
The attached document bundle includes materials from the **NEIMARK Hackathon Academy** and the **School of Digital Ethics**.

## My Role

I contributed in two capacities:

- as a mentor in the **NEIMARK Hackathon Academy**;
- as a participant and project team member in the **School of Digital Ethics**.

## Result

The attached PDF confirms three separate recognitions:

- an official **letter of appreciation** for mentorship in the NEIMARK Hackathon Academy;
- a **winner diploma** for victory in the final project competition of the School of Digital Ethics;
- a **participation certificate** for personal contribution to the development of AI ethics.

The diploma states that the winning project focused on creating a realistic and applicable set of digital ethics rules for the use of artificial intelligence.
